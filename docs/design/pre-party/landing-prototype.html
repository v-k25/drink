Questions

Are bookings live?
Not yet. Join the list and we’ll email you when they are.
Who confirms my booking?
The venue. You get your pass once they do.
What will it cost?
Each venue sets its own prices. You’ll see the full price before you pay.
Can I use the tools without an account?
Yes. Party Planner and Find My Drink are free and don’t need a login.
Does Socifer sell alcohol or show stock?
No. We don’t sell or deliver alcohol, and we don’t know what shops have in stock.
Who is Socifer for?
Adults in Jaipur going out with friends, drinking or not. Please drink responsibly and never drink and drive.

Privacy

We keep your email and choices only to send launch updates for Jaipur. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
